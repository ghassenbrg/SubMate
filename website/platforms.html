Supported platforms

Three streaming services, one translation overlay.

SubMate supports Netflix, TVer, and Prime Video through independent adapters on a shared playback and subtitle core.

Netflix

SubMate supports text subtitle tracks exposed by Netflix, including TTML/DFXP and WebVTT. It does not translate subtitle imagery.

TVer

SubMate joins the segmented HLS WebVTT captions available to the authorized TVer player, then merges and de-duplicates cues into an episode track.

Amazon Prime Video

SubMate supports pages served from primevideo.com and the currently declared Amazon storefront Video pages. It remains limited to the Video path so it does not run across unrelated Amazon pages.

Common requirements

  • Desktop Google Chrome 138 or later.
  • An active subscription and access to the title.
  • A text subtitle track for the title and region.

Firefox, Safari, Edge, and mobile browsers are not current release targets. Catalog, caption availability, and languages vary by title and region.
